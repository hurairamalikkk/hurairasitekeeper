Why a website needs regular care

A website is not something you build once and forget. Software behind it changes constantly, and visitors and hackers both notice when it falls behind. Here is what usually goes wrong without maintenance.

🛡️

It gets hacked

WordPress, themes and plugins release updates all the time, and many of them fix security holes. Attackers scan the internet for sites still running old versions. A hacked site can show spam, redirect visitors or be taken offline completely.

🐢

It slows down

Over time, images get heavier, the database fills with clutter and plugins pile up. Slow pages make visitors leave and can push you down in Google results, which means fewer customers finding you.

💥

Things quietly break

A contact form stops sending emails, a checkout button fails, or a page looks wrong on phones. Nobody tells you, so you lose enquiries and sales for weeks before you notice.

💾

No way back

If a bad update or an attack damages your site and there’s no backup, you may lose your content, your products and your design. A good backup turns a disaster into a short delay.

What each part of the service means

Maintenance can sound vague, so here is exactly what each item in the plans below involves, in plain English.

Updates

I update WordPress itself, your theme and your plugins (or the apps and theme on Shopify and Wix). Updates fix bugs and security problems, but they can also break things if applied carelessly, so I take a backup first and check your site afterwards.

Backups

A backup is a full saved copy of your site, kept away from your hosting so it survives even if the server fails. Weekly means a copy every week. Daily means a copy every day, so you’d never lose more than a day of changes.

Uptime alerts

An automatic tool checks your site every few minutes and emails me if it stops loading. I’m not watching a screen all day, so I don’t promise round-the-clock monitoring, but I see the alert and respond within your plan’s reply time, often before customers complain.

Security scan and cleanup

I scan your site for known vulnerabilities and suspicious files. On plans that include cleanup, I also remove malware and repair the damage if your site is infected.

Speed optimization

I compress images, clear database clutter, review heavy plugins and tune caching so pages load faster. Faster sites keep visitors longer and tend to do better in search.

Edit time

Hours you can use for changes: updating text, swapping images, adding a page, changing a banner, and similar jobs. Hours reset every month and don’t roll over. Bigger projects like redesigns are quoted separately.

Checkout and payment tests

For stores, I place test orders to confirm customers can add to cart, pay and receive confirmation emails. A broken checkout silently costs you sales, so this is checked on a schedule.

Product edits

For stores, I add or update products for you: titles, descriptions, photos, prices and stock. The plan lists how many products are included each month.

Staging copy

A private duplicate of your site. On higher plans I test updates there first, so any problem shows up on the copy instead of your live site.

SEO health check

A monthly look at the basics search engines care about: page titles, broken links, missing descriptions and indexing problems. You get a short list of what I fixed or recommend.

Monthly report

A summary of what I did, what I found and what I suggest next. The short version covers the essentials. The plain-English version explains every item so you always know what you’re paying for.

Support reply time

How quickly I respond to your messages during my working hours (Pakistan time). It’s a response time, not a fixing time. Bigger problems take longer, but you’ll always hear back within your plan’s window.

Do you need access to my website?

Yes. I need an admin login for your site and access to your hosting if possible. You should create a separate account for me, so you can remove my access any time without changing your own password.

Which platforms do you work with?

WordPress and WooCommerce are my main focus. I also work with Shopify and Wix Studio for edits, product updates, apps and fixes. On those platforms the company handles hosting and core updates, so my work focuses on your apps, theme, content and store settings.

What counts as an edit?

Small, specific changes such as updating text, replacing images, adding a page, changing a banner or button, or adjusting a menu. If a request is too big for your plan’s hours, I’ll tell you before I start and offer a fixed quote.

What if something breaks?

I restore from a backup if needed and fix the problem. If it was caused by an update I made, the fix is free and doesn’t use your edit hours.

Can you fix my site if it’s already broken or hacked?

Usually yes. Tell me what’s happening and I’ll look at it for free before recommending anything. Cleanup of a hacked site is included on plans that mention it.

I’m in a different time zone. Is that a problem?

I’m based in Lahore, Pakistan (UTC+5). Because most maintenance happens in the background, the time difference rarely matters. I reply during my working hours, and your plan states how fast: from within 24 hours on the starter plans to within 4 hours on the top plans. I’m not available around the clock, but I respond quickly and take urgent problems seriously.

Is there a contract?

How do I pay?

You’re invoiced in US dollars each month, or once a year if you choose the yearly option. I’ll confirm the available payment methods when we start.
